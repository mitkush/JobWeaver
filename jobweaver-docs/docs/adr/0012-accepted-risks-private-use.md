# Accepted risks for private use, to revisit before any public launch

JobWeaver is used privately by the founder and a few friends. On that basis the founder accepts three risks that would block a public product: (1) fetching LinkedIn and Naukri through Bright Data and Apify breaches those sites' terms of service, which is a civil (contract) risk, not a criminal one; (2) free AI tiers may use prompts, which contain resume content, to improve the provider's products, and some restrict free use for users in certain regions; (3) every free tier can change or disappear without notice.

**Consequences:** these are recorded, not re-debated, during V1; the fallback design (ADR-0003, ADR-0007) limits the impact of (3); a **pre-launch review** of (1) and (2), plus Google verification for Gmail (ADR-0006), is a required gate before JobWeaver is offered publicly or for money.
